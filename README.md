A one-star review written by a houseplant about its human owner. Every
time a friend visits, the owner snips off a cutting and calls it "sharing
the love" — until the plant is down to three leaves and a stump, while its
own severed arm gets potted, named Hope, and watered more than the plant
itself. One line, under 280 characters.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline)
and verifies it is byte-identical, 1–279 characters, a single line, and not
only whitespace.

## Entry

> One star. Every time a friend visits, he snips a cutting off me and calls it sharing the love. I'm down to three leaves and a stump, and he potted my arm yesterday, named it Hope, and waters it more than me.
